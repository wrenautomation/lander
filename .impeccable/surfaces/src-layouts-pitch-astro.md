# Pitch pages (/ and /recruiting)

Mode: Persuade. Light only. Both pitch pages share Pitch.astro and pitch.css.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The pitch set like a partner's letter from a serious firm: a strict 12-column grid, hairline rules, a book serif that speaks and a quiet grotesque that works, two blocks of ink that carry the calls. It refuses the SaaS landing page: no floating pill nav, no glow, no dashboard mock, no cards, no eyebrows.

OWN-WORLD: Bond paper #FAF9F6, second stock #F1EEE7, warm ink #17150F, grey #5B574E for secondary text, rules in ink at low alpha. Ink fills the form panel and the closing screen, with paper-colored buttons inside them; ink buttons on paper. Oxide #A63D17 marks only: woken contacts, the steps rule, the selected-answer dot, small tags, focus. Newsreader for headings, lede, figures and questions, italic for the punch; Hanken Grotesk for text and UI; small uppercase labels for table keys and buttons. Portraits in greyscale. Sections join on rules into one continuous sheet.

STORY: An owner of a midsize recruiting firm sees who it's for, the pain (past clients gone quiet, BD hanging on one or two people), what Wren does and the free pilot, then answers question 1 without scrolling. Everything below is evidence for the doubtful.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: top rule with bird mark and Apply. Cols 1-7: the serif headline with an italic punch near 5.9rem, the ICP-plus-pain lede, what Wren does, William's portrait and name. Cols 8-12: a full-height ink panel: the offer in one line, then question 1 with its choices. Phone: headline, lede, then the ink panel starting inside the first screen with question 1 visible.

FORM: Code-led landing page, assigned direction "The Annual Report" (position 1 of the ordered list), seed key 91fda531. Signature interaction: the dormant list, outlined grey contacts that fill oxide as you scroll; the steps rule fills oxide the same way.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
