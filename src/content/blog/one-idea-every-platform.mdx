I post on six kinds of feed, and all of it starts from one idea, cut to fit each place.

<Fan title="One idea, one week" from="One idea">

- **Shorts and Reels**: 3 a day
- **LinkedIn video**: 1 to 2 a day
- **Carousels**: 1 a day
- **X**: up to 3 posts a day
- **YouTube**: 2 to 3 long videos a week
- **Reddit**: 2 posts a week

</Fan>

Reddit is the odd one out. There I lead with something useful and keep the ask small.

## How one idea becomes a week

<Steps>

1. I write the idea down. Usually it's something I learned that week building Wren.
2. It goes through Gemini first.
3. Then through Claude.
4. Then I edit it myself. Nothing goes out in a voice that isn't mine.

</Steps>

## Posting is half of it

Posting gets you seen. The other half is showing up where other people are talking:

- Connecting with people I'd like to work with
- Commenting when I have something to add
- Replying to comments on my own posts
- A short welcome message when someone connects
- Quoting posts I have a take on
- Replying when someone mentions me
- Answering questions in subreddits
- Pinning the best comment

<Stat n="1,000">
touches a week. That's my estimate for the plan above, posts and replies together.
</Stat>

## The hours

By hand, this is about seven hours a day of clicking around eight platforms. Software now does the posting and lines up the replies, so I spend one to three. The ideas are still mine.
